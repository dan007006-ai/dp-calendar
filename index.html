DP Calendar

Your 28-day deployment period on your phone — days off, pay days, holidays, notes, hours and time banks, with a booklet view that looks like the printed page.

Getting started

  1. Choose sworn or civilian.
  2. Pick your schedule (4/10, 5/40, 3/12 or 9/17).
  3. Tap your regular days off once — they repeat every DP.
  4. Optionally enter your sick bank balances. You can skip this and add them later under Time banks.
  5. Tap any day to set a code, hours, overtime, a note or a reminder.

Common questions

Where is my data? Only on your phone. Use Settings › Backup to save a copy.

How do I get it into Outlook or Google Calendar? Menu › Export to calendar › Add to phone calendar. If Outlook or Google is set up on your phone, the events show there too.

Booklet view? Tap Booklet under the DP header; your phone turns sideways and shows the page like the printed booklet. Every box is the same size: tap one to see and edit the whole day. Tap NOTES to read every note for the DP, or PLANNING to write your plans.
