DP Calendar

Your 28-day deployment period on your phone — days off, pay days, holidays, notes, hours and time banks, with a booklet view that looks like the printed page.

Getting started

  1. Pick your schedule (4/10, 5/40, 3/12, 9/17 or civilian).
  2. Tap your regular days off once — they repeat every DP.
  3. Tap any day to set a code, hours, overtime, a note or a reminder.

Common questions

Where is my data? Only on your phone. Use Settings › Backup to save a copy.

How do I get it into Outlook or Google Calendar? Menu › Export to calendar › Add to phone calendar. If Outlook or Google is set up on your phone, the events show there too.

Booklet view? Tap Booklet under the DP header; your phone turns sideways and shows the page like the printed booklet.
