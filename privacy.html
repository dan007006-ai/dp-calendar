DP Calendar

Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026

DP Calendar keeps everything on your phone. We don't collect, store, sell or share your information, and the app has no accounts, ads or analytics.

What the app stores

The days off, day codes, hours, overtime, notes, time-bank balances and settings you enter are saved only on your device, inside the app. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

Permissions

  • Calendar — used only when you tap Add to phone calendar. The app creates a calendar named "DP Calendar" on your device and writes your days off, pay days, holidays and notes into it. It does not read, upload or change your other calendars. You can remove that calendar from inside the app at any time.
  • Notifications — used only for the reminders you switch on (days off due, and reminders on specific days). They are scheduled on the device.

Files you share

When you choose Save backup or Share .ics file, the app creates a file and hands it to your phone's share sheet. Where that file goes (Files, email, cloud storage) is your choice and is governed by the app you pick.

Internet

DP Calendar works offline. It does not send your data over the internet.

Children

DP Calendar is a work-schedule tool meant for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
